Gentle First-Aid

Suddenly got a weird feeling?

🌿 Change your physical environment

If possible:

  • Sit somewhere comfortable.
  • Loosen your shoulders and jaw.
  • Get some fresh air.
  • Sip some water.
  • Wash your face with cool water.
  • Put your phone down for a few minutes.

🧠 Don't fight the feeling

Instead of “Why am I feeling this?! Something must be wrong!”, try:

“I'm experiencing a strong uncomfortable feeling. I don't have to solve it this second. I'll give myself a few minutes to settle.”

You don't have to identify the emotion immediately.

✍️ Do a 30-second check-in

Ask yourself:

  • What am I feeling?
  • What was happening just before it started?
  • What does my body need right now?
  • What's one small thing I can do in the next 5 minutes?

Sometimes the answer is simply water, food, rest, fresh air, a quiet place, or talking to someone.

🎧 Give your brain something gentle to focus on

Try:

Calm music Rain/ocean sounds Coloring A simple puzzle Organizing something Taking a short walk Talking to someone you trust

🤝 Tell someone

If the feeling is strong or keeps returning, you don't have to handle it by yourself. You could simply say:

“I'm feeling really weird and anxious right now. I don't know why. Can you stay with me for a bit?”

And if you have severe or unusual physical symptoms, feel physically unsafe, or the feeling isn't settling, tell a trusted adult and seek appropriate medical help.

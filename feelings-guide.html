Feeling Anxious

Racing thoughts, physical tightness, or anxious feelings

Anxiety can feel intense in your body, but remember: you are safe right now, and this feeling is temporary.

3 Gentle Steps to Try Now

  1. Drop your shoulders away from your ears and unclamp your jaw.
  2. Try the 4-7-8 Breathing Technique: Inhale for 4s, hold for 7s, exhale for 8s.
  3. Press your feet firmly onto the floor. Feel the solid ground holding you up.

Recommended Micro-Action

Find 3 round objects in the room and touch them slowly, noticing their physical texture.

Gentle First-Aid

Suddenly got a weird feeling?

Change your physical environment

If possible:

  • Sit somewhere comfortable.
  • Loosen your shoulders and jaw.
  • Get some fresh air.
  • Sip some water.
  • Wash your face with cool water.
  • Put your phone down for a few minutes.

Do a 30-second check-in

Ask yourself:

  • What am I feeling?
  • What was happening just before it started?
  • What does my body need right now?
  • What's one small thing I can do in the next 5 minutes?

Sometimes the answer is simply water, food, rest, fresh air, a quiet place, or talking to someone.
